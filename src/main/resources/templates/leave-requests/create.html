Automated Validation Rules
  • Holiday Exclusion: Official company holidays within your requested range are automatically excluded from the leave duration calculation.
  • Balance Pre-Check: The platform verifies that your available balance for the leave year covers the requested working days before allowing submission.
  • No Instant Deductions: Leave balances are never debited upon submission; balance deduction is performed atomically upon approval.
  • Conflict Prevention: Overlapping pending or approved leave requests for the same employee are automatically rejected.
  • Policy Rules: Maximum consecutive days and minimum service days defined by the applicable leave policy are enforced.
Approval Routing

Requests are automatically assigned to your active Reporting Manager. If no manager is configured, requests route to the HR Department approver pool.
